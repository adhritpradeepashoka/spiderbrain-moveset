# Evidence behind the priors
- Steinhoff et al. 2020, *Marpissa muscosa* visual neuropils: AME→AM1(TZ1–4)→AM2→arcuate body; ALE/PLE→AL1/PL1→AL2/PL2 and shared L2→mushroom body; PME→PM1→AB; mushroom-body bridge. Tier A.
- Land 1969: AME retina, under 1500 receptors per eye, 4 tiers. Nagata et al. 2012 (Science 335:469): green defocus in layer 2 encodes distance.
- Babu & Barth 1984, *Cupiennius salei*: ~50,900 brain + ~49,000 suboesophageal cells, the only real count; salticid totals (≈100k Phidippus, ≈600k Portia) are anecdotal.
- Hylyphantes graminicola single-cell atlas (GEO GSE241696): a web-builder; transmitter priors are placeholders until it is processed.
- Jump data: Dryad *Maratus splendens* kinematics (doi:10.5061/dryad.hdr7sqvsp).
Every number in `configs/` beyond these facts is my own prior. Verify citations before reuse.
