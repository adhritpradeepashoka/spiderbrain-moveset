# Contest checklist (BE UNLIMITED Crab Robot Open-source Challenge)

Dates from press/README coverage, not from official rules text I could read: 30 Sep - 30 Nov 2026; Round 1 closes **30 Oct**. Verify on the official page (beunlimited.me) first.

1. **Eligibility.** Look for country, age and shipping terms. I found none stated for India or minors. If there is an age limit, enter through a parent/guardian's account and ask them to read the rules.
2. **Video > 15 s** on X or YouTube, with `#Jumper #CrabRobot #OpenSourceRobot` and a mention of the official account (check its handle on the rules page).
3. **Say what is synthetic.** Caption it: "Synthetic jumping-spider brain prior (no real salticid connectome exists) steering Jumper's own policies." Judging is 60% audience, 40% experts on appearance, playfulness, usefulness; honesty also protects you from a plagiarism/overclaim complaint.
4. **Music** you use must be licensed or your own.
5. **Show the brain.** Overlay or cut-in the `[spiderbrain]` log lines or a region activity plot so viewers see something spider-like drives the robot.
6. Link the GitHub repo (AGPL-3.0; Jumper is Apache-2.0, keep its notices if you copy anything).
7. If the prize needs a reply, answer within the stated window (reported as 5 days).
